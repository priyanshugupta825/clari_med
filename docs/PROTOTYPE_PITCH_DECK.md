### Slide 2: The Problem: Fragmented Health Records
* **Records Scattered Everywhere**: Patient medical history is spread across multiple unconnected hospitals, clinics, and paper folders.
* **Hard to Access When It Matters**: Prescriptions and lab reports get lost, preventing doctors from seeing past medical context.
* **No Unified Health View**: Disconnected records lead to missed chronic diseases, redundant blood tests, and ER blindspots.

---

### Slide 3: Meet ClariMed (Working Prototype)
* **What it is**: An AI-powered Personal Health Record (PHR) ecosystem fully integrated with India\'s Ayushman Bharat Digital Mission (ABDM).
* **Key Pillars**:
  1. Securely store and access all health data in one unified place.
  2. Built on ABDM-compliant national digital health infrastructure (ABHA).
  3. Multimodal AI that extracts and structures medical data automatically.
* **Live Link**: [https://clarimed-s1a7.vercel.app/](https://clarimed-s1a7.vercel.app/)

---

### Slide 4: Key Features — Your Health, Unified
* **ABHA ID Integration**: Unique 14-digit digital health identity connecting patients to the entire national network.
* **Longitudinal Health Timeline**: Complete health history from every provider organized chronologically.
* **Secure Cloud Storage**: Fully ABDM & DPDP Act compliant with PostgreSQL Row Level Security (RLS).

---

### Slide 5: What You Can Do
1. **Health Vault (Multimodal Gemini AI Parser)**: Upload any prescription image or lab PDF $\rightarrow$ AI auto-extracts doctor, medicines, dosages, and biomarkers.
2. **Manage Consent**: Generate 15-minute PIN-protected temporary consultation links with full audit logging.
3. **Emergency QR Pass**: 1-click printable emergency pass for first responders (blood group, severe allergies, SOS contacts).

---

### Slide 6: How It Works (User Journey)
$$\text{Create Account (ABHA Linked)} \longrightarrow \text{Upload / Link Records} \longrightarrow \text{AI Structuring \& Timeline} \longrightarrow \text{Share Securely with Doctors}$$

---

### Slide 7: Why ClariMed? (The Novelty)
* **AI-Powered Health Insights**: Gemini AI summarizes complex lab panels and prescriptions in plain language.
* **Cross-Center Early Warning (JMIR 2024)**: Detects overlooked chronic disease trajectories (e.g. CKD Stage 3) up to **434 days earlier** across fragmented visits.
* **Your Data, Your Control**: Complete patient ownership with instant revocation and access audit logs.
